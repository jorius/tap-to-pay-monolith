What was built (branch feat/statement-recurring, PR #4 on top of PR #3):
- Service, statement: GET /vendors/:id/statement.pdf?month=YYYY-MM&lang=es|en, rendered with pdfkit 0.20.2, two A4 pages per receiving wallet with activity. Page 1 is payment capacity over the last 6 months: average and lowest monthly income, income stability (coefficient of variation), average monthly obligations (the vendor's recurring payments), obligations to income, estimated payment capacity per month, a month-by-month table (income, sales, days with sales, obligations paid, net flow, payments on time), a chart of income against obligations, the payment history and this month's obligations. Page 2 is the month's sales: revenue, approved sales, average and median ticket, customers, approval rate and refusals, change against last month, best day, busiest hour, channels, revenue per day and a 6-month trend. Every page carries a verification code (SHA-256 of the figures) and a note that it is not a bank certificate.
- Service, recurring payments: a catalog of Colombian services (EPM, Claro, Tigo, Movistar, ETB, Vanti) at GET /billers, each receiving into a wallet from BILLER_WALLETS or BILLERS_DEFAULT_WALLET, plus any Interledger wallet the vendor types. The vendor owns the payments (prompt 3 moved them from the buyer): it pays from its own wallets through a payer identity (Vendor.payerBuyerId, a buyer holding the vendor's addresses, hidden from GET /buyers) authorized once with npm run grant:buyer. Routes under /vendors/:id/payer and /vendors/:id/recurring (list, enrol, pause or resume, remove, pay now). A scheduler pays each one on its day at 08:00 local, claiming the month before charging so two ticks never charge twice; each run is an ordinary sale to a BILLER vendor through chargeSale, with chargedVia RECURRING.
- Service, movements: GET /vendors/:id/sales returns the vendor's recurring payments as OUT movements next to the IN sales (direction=in|out|all).
- Migrations: 20261017120000_recurring_payments (Vendor.kind, RecurringPayment) and 20261017150000_vendor_recurring (Vendor.payerBuyerId, RecurringPayment.vendorId).
- App: a "Pagos" tab in the vendor menu with the enrolled services, the command to authorize the wallet while it is missing, and an "Add payment" sheet (catalog grid and form; Back always visible since prompt 4). Services are drawn as badges in their brand colour with their initials; the real logos are trademarks and are not shipped. The statement card sits on the vendor's Panel (moved from Movimientos in prompt 5). Movimientos lists payments out with a minus sign and the service paid, with an All / Sales / Payments filter (prompt 6).

Verification: npm run typecheck and npm test in tap-to-pay-svc (183 tests green); tsc, expo lint and an iOS export of tap-to-pay-app. A sample statement with six months of sales and bills (one missed) was rendered and checked page by page. Against the local Postgres and service: both migrations, the vendor's payer identity (EUR wallet, not yet authorized), the PDF (200 application/pdf), an enrolment resolved against the Test Wallet, and a recurring payment listed as an OUT movement. Not yet run on phones: authorizing the vendor's wallet, a scheduled run, and downloading the PDF.
