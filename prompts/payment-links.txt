What was built (branch feat/payment-links, then merged with feat/qr-whatsapp-pay into feat/payments-qr-whatsapp):
- Service: a sale AWAITING_TAP can also be charged through a payment link. POST /sales/:id/payment-link returns a random 12-character code (never the sale id) that expires after PAYMENT_LINK_TTL_MINUTES, the link, the app link, the QR (GET /pay/:code/qr.svg), a wa.me URL and a detailed WhatsApp message (amount in the local format, description, reference, date, expiry, how to pay, a safety note), optionally to a phone number. GET /pay/:code and POST /pay/:code/charge reuse chargeSale unchanged; sales gain description, payCode, payCodeExpiresAt and chargedVia (NFC or LINK).
- From feat/qr-whatsapp-pay: the link page GET /p/:code also lets the buyer pay in the browser, with no app, by typing their wallet address (remembered on the phone); PUBLIC_URL (proxy headers honoured when unset); the vendor creates sales on a phone without NFC; tunnel headers on the QR image.
- App: the vendor terminal shows the QR in a modal, sends the link by WhatsApp to a typed number or any contact, shares it, and follows a sale paid through the link. The buyer has "Pay with a QR or link" (camera scanner or pasted link) and a payment screen that pays with one of their authorized wallets.
